Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. A single burst pipe can release 4 to 8 gallons of water per minute, which is 240 to 480 gallons per hour flooding through your walls, ceilings and floors. The trigger may be a hard freeze, corroded or aging lines, high pressure or physical damage, but the need is the same: act immediately so water does less harm to the structure, the floors, your possessions and the building's systems.

When a pipe bursts in Tempe or the surrounding area, Tempe Restoration runs an emergency response around the clock. Our restoration technicians can help you locate and shut off your water supply, and we coordinate with licensed plumbers so the plumbing repair is done properly. Once the water source is stopped, we immediately begin extraction with truck-mounted and portable equipment, removing standing water from all affected areas as quickly as possible.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. Our thermal imaging and moisture detection equipment traces water migration paths so every affected area is identified and properly dried, which prevents the mold growth and structural deterioration that hidden moisture causes.

What a Professional Brings to Burst Pipe Repair

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. Hidden spaces may hold established mold by the time you notice, with structural materials seriously weakened as well. Our technicians find all the wet areas at the start by tracing migration with thermal imaging and meter readings.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. Truck-mounted extractors remove water hundreds of times faster than a shop vacuum, and commercial dehumidifiers pull 10 to 20 times more moisture from the air than residential units. With only 24 to 48 hours before mold starts, you need equipment this fast and this strong.

Warning Signs for Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • No water at a tap in freezing weather, a sign the line is frozen
  • A musty smell near a wall, cabinet or floor with plumbing behind it
  • Frost or ice on exposed pipes during cold weather
  • Faucets and showers across the building suddenly running weak
  • Wet spots, bulging or staining on walls or ceilings
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak
  • Unexplained standing water along baseboards or in the middle of a room

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Unheated spaces like garages, attics and crawl spaces need pipe insulation on exposed lines
  • If your home still has polybutylene or aging galvanized steel lines, replace them before one gives out
  • Everyone in the house should be able to locate and turn the main water valve in a hurry
  • Outdoor faucets should be closed, drained and free of hoses ahead of freezing weather
  • During freezing weather, keep faucets dripping and cabinet doors open so heat can circulate
  • Never shut the furnace off completely during a winter trip, or pipes inside may freeze
