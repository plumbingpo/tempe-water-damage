Moisture Monitoring

Risk Assessments That Help Prevent Commercial Water Damage

The law expects owners of commercial buildings to keep their properties in safe condition, known as a duty of care. Injuries, lawsuits, and damaged property can all trace back to a sudden failure from inadequate waterproofing, concealed moisture, or plumbing past its prime. A Tempe Restoration risk assessment looks at every part of the property for exposure to water intrusion, with moisture monitoring tools and thermal imaging doing the detection. Findings are delivered in writing, with risks identified, priorities assigned, and specific prevention measures recommended.

These assessments serve a dual purpose: they protect people and property by identifying vulnerabilities, and they demonstrate due diligence that can protect you legally if an incident occurs. Courts and insurance companies look favorably on property owners who maintain regular professional inspections and act on expert recommendations. Every commercial property should have an annual moisture assessment, and high-risk areas, including aging plumbing systems, basements, and mechanical rooms, should be inspected more often.
